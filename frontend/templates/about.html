01 The Idea, in One Page

Every student collects proof of who they are becoming. A certificate from a workshop attended on a Saturday. A hackathon that ran for thirty-six hours and ended in a photograph nobody printed. A medal from an inter-college tournament. A paper that finally got accepted. An internship offer letter. A first prize at a coding contest. Individually they are small; together they are the entire story of a student's four years.

Today that story lives in the worst possible place — a phone gallery, a WhatsApp forward, an e-mail thread, a plastic folder that comes out once a year. When a resume, scholarship form, placement drive, or accreditation report needs evidence, the hunt begins from zero every single time.

The Student Achievement Portal replaces that hunt with a system. A student uploads an achievement once, attaches proof, and picks its category. A faculty member reviews it and approves or returns it with a reason. From that moment, the record is permanent, searchable, and trusted.

The 4-Layer Architecture

  • Device Layer: Phones, tablets, and lab machines all receive the same lightweight HTML. Fast even on weak campus Wi-Fi.
  • Presentation: Server-rendered Thymeleaf templates styled by hand-written vanilla CSS. Zero bulky JS bundles.
  • Application: Spring Security filter answers authentication and authorization before controllers run. Controllers validate, Services enforce business logic, Repositories talk to MySQL.
  • Persistence: Relational schema over MySQL (or H2 in MySQL mode). Proof documents stored in an isolated, secure File Vault with SHA-256 checksum deduplication. Append-only verification log.
